AnimationStudio Uploader

A private publishing tool for one creator's own channels.

AnimationStudio Uploader is a small, self-hosted tool used by the operator of the AnimationStudio channel to publish short animated videos that the operator creates, and to review how those videos perform.

What the tool does

  • Uploads finished videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and own TikTok account, only after the operator has manually approved each video.
  • Reads basic information and performance statistics (views, watch time, likes) of the operator's own videos to produce private reports for the operator.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the operator's own accounts are connected. No other user can sign in, and no data of other users is collected.

Platform services used

  • YouTube API Services (see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy)
  • TikTok for Developers: Login Kit and Content Posting API
